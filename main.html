01 About

A systems mindset, grounded in the physical world.

I care about the full path from an idea on paper to a prototype I can measure, question, and improve.

I like the space between theory and something that actually works.

My interests sit where circuits meet software: sensing a physical signal, making a decision under real constraints, and producing an output that is useful in the real world.

As an electrical and electronic engineering student, I’m building depth across embedded systems, electronics, signals, and software. I value clear assumptions, honest measurements, and documentation that makes the next iteration easier.
